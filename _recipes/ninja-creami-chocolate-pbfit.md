title: Ninja Creami Chocolate PBFit

tags: ninja-creami
recipeYield: 4

ingredients:
- 1 bottle chocolate Fairlife
- 2 tbsp PBFit
- 1 tbsp Jello vanilla sugar free
- 1 tsp vanilla extract

directions:
- Combine ingredients
- Hit it with a stick blender
- Freeze for 24 hours
- Spin 2 times on "Light Ice Cream" setting
