title: Cranberry Sauce
image: cranberry-sauce.jpg
tags: thanksgiving sides
recipeYield: 8

ingredients:
- 12 oz fresh cranberries
- 2 apples
- 1 cup sugar
- 1/2 cup orange juice
- 1 tablespoon orange zest
- 1/2 teaspoon salt

directions:
- Core, peel and cut apples into 1/4 in cubes.
- Combine cranberries, apples, white sugar, orange juice, orange zest, and salt in a medium saucepan.
- Bring to a boil over medium-high heat. Cook, stirring occasionally, until berries start to pop, about 5 minutes.
- Press berries against side of pan with wooden spoon and continue to cook until berries have broken down and sauce thickens to a jam-like consistency, about 5 minutes more.
- Remove from heat and let cool for 30 minutes.
- Adjust consistency with water as needed.
- Serve immediately or place in an airtight container and store in the refrigerator.
- Serve cold or warm.
