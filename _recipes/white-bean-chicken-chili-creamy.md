title: White Bean Chicken Chili (Creamy)

tags: dinner soup
recipeYield: 7

ingredients:
- 1 pound rotisserie chicken, shredded
- 1 medium chopped onion
- 1 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- 2 cans (15-ounce) Great Northern beans, drained and rinsed
- 14 1/2 ounces chicken broth
- 2 cans diced green chiles (4-ounce cans)
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1 teaspoon dried oregano
- 1 teaspoon ground cumin
- 1/2 teaspoon pepper
- 1/4 teaspoon cayenne pepper
- 1 cup sour cream
- 1/2 cup cream
- Toppings Green onion, Tortilla chips, shredded cheese

directions:
- In a large saucepan, saute the onion and garlic powder with the olive oil until the onions are translucent.
- Add in beans, broth, chiles, and seasonings. Bring to a boil. Reduce heat to simmer and cook for another 30 minutes, uncovered.
- Add in shredded chicken.
- Remove from heat. Stir in cream and sour cream.
- Top with shredded cheese, tortilla chips, green onions, lime wedges, or sliced jalapenos.
- ----- Slow Cooker Instructions -----
- Cook the onions and garlic powder in the olive oil in a saucepan over medium-high heat for a few minutes. Add to the crockpot.
- Add the rest of the ingredients to the slow cooker, except for sour cream, heavy cream, and toppings.
- Slow cook the white chili chicken on low for 5 hours or high for 3 hours.
- Stir in sour cream and heavy cream.
- Ladle the white bean chili recipe into bowls and add your favorite toppings.

components:

---
To thicken the soup, blend one of the drained, rinsed cans of beans before adding to the chili.
