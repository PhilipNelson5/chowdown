title: Ninja Creami Dole Whip
image: ninja-creami-dole-whip.jpg
tags: ninja-creami
recipeYield: 4

ingredients:
- 1 can of pineapple chunks in pineapple juice
- Milk of choice or vanilla protein shake
- 2 tbsp of Jello sugar free vanilla pudding powder

directions:
- Fill your pint container 1/3 full with pineapple juice.
- Fill another 1/3 of the container with milk of choice
- Add Jello sugar free vanilla pudding powder
- Add in pineapple chunks to the max fill line.
- Hit it with a stick blender
- Freeze for 24 hours
- Spin 2 times on "Light Ice Cream" setting
