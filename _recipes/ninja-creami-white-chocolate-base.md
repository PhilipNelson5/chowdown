ingredients:
- 1 vanilla Fairlife protein shake
- 40 g Monkfruit
- 2 1/2 tablespoons zero sugar white chocolate jello
- 1/16 teaspoon xanthum gum
- Pinch of salt
- 1/2 teaspoon vanilla bean paste

directions:
- Combine and stir or blend thoroughly
- Freeze for 24 hours
- Spin on "Light Ice Cream" setting
