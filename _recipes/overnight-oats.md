title: Overnight Oats
image: overnight-oats.jpg
tags: breakfast
recipeYield: 6

ingredients:
- 1 1/3 cup greek yogurt (plain or vanilla)
- 2 2/3 cup milk (choose your favorite kind)
- 4 Tbsp chia seeds or ground flax seeds
- 2 pinches salt
- 1/4 cup honey
- 2 cup heaping rolled oats
- 2 tsp vanilla (skip if using vanilla yogurt)

directions:
- Mix it all together and store in a sealed container in the fridge overnight.

components:

---
Good for about 6 days after making
